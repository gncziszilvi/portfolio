THE FOUNDATION OF MY PROJECT IS THE MYSTERIOUS ATMOSPHERE OF THE SWAMP

2025

VEIL
OF FOG

A projektem alapjául az „Ahol a folyami rákok énekelnek” című film szolgált, amelynek sejtelmes mocsári atmoszféráját fogalmaztam meg.

During the pattern design process, my aim was to capture the lush flora and mysterious atmosphere of the wetlands. I achieved the enigmatic, misty mood seen in the film by playing with layering and opacity. The density within the patterns and the intertwining motifs reflect the organic growth of nature and the vibrant wildlife of the swamp. The color palette was drawn directly from the visual world of the film, dominated by deep greens, earth tones, and watery bluish-grays.
